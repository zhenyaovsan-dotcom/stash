# On-chain basics

Blockchain data can help you investigate a token, but it cannot guarantee an outcome.

## Wallets

Look at funding sources, transaction history and interactions with other addresses. A shared funder can indicate a connection, but does not prove common ownership.

## Holder distribution

Check how much supply is held by the largest addresses.

- Exchange and liquidity pool addresses can distort rankings.
- One person can use multiple wallets.
- Holder percentages change as tokens move.

## Liquidity

Liquidity affects how easily a position can be sold without substantial price impact. Check the pool, available liquidity and whether liquidity can be removed.

## Developer activity

Review previous launches and the deployer's transactions. Look for repeated patterns, but distinguish evidence from speculation.

## The rule

Never treat a single metric as proof that a token is safe or doomed.
