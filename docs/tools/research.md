# Research tools

Research before you trade.

## What to investigate

- **Token:** liquidity, market cap and trading activity
- **Holders:** concentration and linked wallets
- **Developer:** previous launches and wallet history
- **Liquidity:** amount, status and ability to withdraw
- **Transactions:** suspicious funding patterns and coordinated activity

## Research checklist

1. Verify the token address.
2. Inspect liquidity and holder concentration.
3. Review the developer wallet.
4. Check whether activity looks organic.
5. Decide how much you can afford to lose.

No single signal proves that a token is safe.
